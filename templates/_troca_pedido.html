{# ── Novo pedido de troca — the one way to ask for a swap ───────────────────── Included by /minha-escala (opened from a shift card, the shift already chosen) and by /trocas ("Novo pedido", which starts by choosing the shift). Donations are published to the whole team without nominating a recipient. A direct request to a colleague always exchanges two shifts. What it knows before sending, so a request is not refused two people later: * which colleagues already work during those hours (from the schedule every employee can already see on /escala); * which of their shifts would leave either person double-booked; * which shifts are already promised in another open request. The server enforces all of it again; this only says it at the right moment. Everything interpolated into innerHTML goes through escHtml() — `nome`, `horario` and week labels are free text. #}
